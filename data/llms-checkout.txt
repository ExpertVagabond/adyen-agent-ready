## POST /applePay/sessions

Get an Apple Pay session. You need to use this endpoint if you have an API-only integration with Apple Pay which uses Adyen's Apple Pay certificate.

- Reference: https://docs.adyen.com/api-explorer/Checkout/72/post/applePay/sessions
- Request body: `ApplePaySessionRequest`
- Required: `displayName` (string), `domainName` (string), `merchantIdentifier` (string)
- Field notes:

  - `domainName`: The domain name you provided when you added Apple Pay in your Customer Area.
  - `merchantIdentifier`: Your merchant identifier registered with Apple Pay.
- Responses: 200 OK - the request has succeeded.
